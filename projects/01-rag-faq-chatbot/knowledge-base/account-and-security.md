# Contoso Pay FAQ: Account & Security

> Contoso Pay is a **fictional** digital wallet used to demonstrate this chatbot.

## How do I open a Contoso Pay account?
Download the Contoso Pay app from the App Store or Google Play, tap **Sign up**, and verify your phone number with the 6-digit code we send by SMS. You must be at least 18 years old.

## What documents do I need to verify my identity?
A valid government-issued photo ID (passport, national ID card, or driver's license) and a selfie taken in the app. Verification usually completes within 10 minutes and always within 24 hours.

## What happens if my account is not verified?
Unverified accounts can receive money but cannot send more than $50 per day or withdraw to a bank account.

## How do I reset my PIN?
Go to **Profile > Security > Reset PIN**. We send a one-time code to your registered phone number. For your safety, a reset PIN cannot be used for transfers for the first 2 hours.

## Does Contoso Pay support two-factor authentication?
Yes. Two-factor authentication (2FA) is on by default. You can use SMS codes or an authenticator app. We recommend an authenticator app.

## What should I do if my phone is lost or stolen?
Call our 24/7 fraud line at +1-800-555-0199 or log in at web.contosopay.example and choose **Freeze account**. Freezing stops all outgoing payments immediately. Your balance stays safe.

## Will Contoso Pay ever ask for my PIN?
No. Contoso Pay staff will never ask for your PIN, password, or one-time codes by phone, email, or chat. Report any such request to security@contosopay.example.

## How do I close my account?
Withdraw your remaining balance, then go to **Profile > Settings > Close account**. Closure takes up to 5 business days. We keep transaction records for 7 years as required by law.
