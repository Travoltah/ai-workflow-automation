# Contoso Pay FAQ: Fees & Limits

> Contoso Pay is a **fictional** digital wallet used to demonstrate this chatbot.

## What fees does Contoso Pay charge?
| Action | Fee |
|---|---|
| Send money to a Contoso Pay user | Free |
| Pay a bill | Free |
| Standard bank withdrawal (1–3 business days) | Free |
| Instant withdrawal to debit card | 1.5% (minimum $0.25, maximum $15) |
| International transfer | 1% plus the exchange-rate margin shown before you confirm |
| Top up by debit card | Free |
| Inactive account (no activity for 12 months) | $2 per month |

## What are the daily and monthly limits?
| Account type | Daily send limit | Monthly send limit |
|---|---|---|
| Unverified | $50 | $200 |
| Verified | $2,500 | $10,000 |
| Business | $25,000 | $100,000 |

## How can I increase my limits?
Verify your identity to move to a Verified account. Business accounts can request higher limits by submitting proof of business registration in **Profile > Business**.

## Is there a minimum balance?
No. There is no minimum balance and no monthly account fee for active accounts.

## Do you charge for receiving money?
No. Receiving money from other Contoso Pay users or bank transfers is always free.
