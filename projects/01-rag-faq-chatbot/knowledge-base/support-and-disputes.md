# Contoso Pay FAQ: Support & Disputes

> Contoso Pay is a **fictional** digital wallet used to demonstrate this chatbot.

## How do I contact customer support?
In the app, go to **Help > Chat with us** (available 7am–10pm Eastern, every day). You can also email support@contosopay.example. We reply to emails within 1 business day.

## I sent money to the wrong person. What can I do?
Contact the recipient first and ask them to send it back. If they don't respond within 3 days, open a dispute from **Activity > select the payment > Report a problem**. We can't guarantee recovery for payments sent to the wrong person.

## How do I dispute a transaction I don't recognise?
Freeze your account immediately (**Profile > Security > Freeze account**), then open the transaction and tap **Report a problem**. Unauthorised transactions reported within 60 days are investigated, and we aim to resolve them within 10 business days.

## What is your refund policy for merchant purchases?
Refunds for purchases are handled by the merchant. Once a merchant issues a refund, it reaches your Contoso Pay balance within 5 business days.

## Is my money protected?
Customer balances are held in segregated accounts at partner banks and are never used for Contoso Pay's own operations.

## How do I make a complaint?
Email complaints@contosopay.example with your account phone number and a description. We acknowledge complaints within 2 business days and send a final response within 15 business days.
