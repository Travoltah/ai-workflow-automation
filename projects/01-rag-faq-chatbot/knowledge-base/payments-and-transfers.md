# Contoso Pay FAQ: Payments & Transfers

> Contoso Pay is a **fictional** digital wallet used to demonstrate this chatbot.

## How do I send money to another Contoso Pay user?
Tap **Send**, enter the recipient's phone number or @username, enter the amount, and confirm with your PIN. Transfers between Contoso Pay users are instant and free.

## Can I send money to someone who doesn't have Contoso Pay?
Yes. Enter their phone number and they receive an SMS link. They have 7 days to claim the money by creating an account. If unclaimed, the money is returned to you automatically.

## How long do bank transfers take?
Standard bank withdrawals arrive in 1 to 3 business days. Instant withdrawals to an eligible debit card arrive within 30 minutes.

## Can I send money internationally?
Yes, to 40 countries. Exchange rates are shown before you confirm, and the rate is locked for 30 minutes. International transfers usually arrive within 1 business day.

## Can I cancel a payment?
Payments to Contoso Pay users cannot be cancelled once sent. Scheduled payments can be cancelled up to 1 hour before they run, from **Activity > Scheduled**.

## How do I pay bills with Contoso Pay?
Tap **Pay bills**, choose the biller (electricity, water, internet, or mobile airtime), and enter your account number. Bill payments are free.

## Why was my payment declined?
The most common reasons are: insufficient balance, exceeding your daily limit, an unverified account, or an automatic fraud check. Open the declined payment in **Activity** to see the exact reason.

## Does Contoso Pay accept credit cards?
You can add a debit card or bank account to top up your wallet. Credit cards are not accepted for top-ups.
